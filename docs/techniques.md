# MENTALMATH — Creator Technique Library

## Verified Creator Mental Calculation Methods

### 1. Internalized Abacus Representation
- **Concept**: Internalized mental feeling/visualization of abacus beads and digit positions.
- **Progression**: Visible Digital Abacus $\rightarrow$ Guided $\rightarrow$ Partial Hidden $\rightarrow$ Flash Exposure $\rightarrow$ Disappearing Exposure $\rightarrow$ Numbers Only $\rightarrow$ Rapid Mental.

### 2. Chunked Addition
- **Concept**: Hold first number mentally and add subsequent numbers in place-value chunks.
- **Example**: $587 + 296 + 413 \rightarrow 587 + 200 (787) \rightarrow +90 (877) \rightarrow +6 (883) \dots$

### 3. Chunked Subtraction
- **Concept**: Maintain running mental minuend and subtract place-value chunks sequentially.
- **Example**: $748 - 567 \rightarrow 748 - 500 (248) \rightarrow -60 (188) \rightarrow -7 (181)$.

### 4. Place-Value Decomposition
- **Concept**: Multiply multi-digit numbers by decomposing the multiplier into exact place values.
- **Example**: $237 \times 24 \rightarrow 237 \times 20 (4740) + 237 \times 4 (948) = 5688$.
- **Clarification**: Explicitly avoids forced rounding shortcuts like $237 \times (40 - 3)$.

### 5. Easy-Number Recognition
- **Concept**: Spot easy target factors ($25, 50, 100, 125, 200, 250, 500, 1000$).
- **Example**: $64 \times 125 \rightarrow 125 \times 60 (7500) + 125 \times 4 (500) = 8000$.

### 6. Table Mastery via Fast Reconstruction
- **Concept**: Fast reconstruction strategy based on base-10 anchors rather than pure memory.
